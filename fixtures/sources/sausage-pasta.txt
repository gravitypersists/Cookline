Orecchiette with Sausage & Greens
Serves 4

1 lb orecchiette
1 lb Italian sausage, casings removed
2 tbsp olive oil
4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
½ tsp chili flakes
1 bunch kale, stems removed, chopped
½ cup grated parmesan
2 tbsp salt

Bring 4 quarts of water to a boil and add the salt. Cook the orecchiette 9 to 11 minutes, until just al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.

Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large skillet and brown the sausage, breaking it up, 6 to 8 minutes until cooked through. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook about 1 minute, until fragrant. Add the kale and cook 3 to 4 minutes until wilted. Add the drained pasta, the pasta water and the parmesan and toss until glossy.
