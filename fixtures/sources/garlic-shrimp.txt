Garlic Butter Shrimp
Serves 2

1 lb shrimp, peeled
3 tbsp butter
5 cloves garlic, minced
1 shallot, thinly sliced
Pinch of chili flakes
½ lemon
2 tbsp chopped parsley

Pat the shrimp dry. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic, shallot and chili flakes and cook 30 seconds, until fragrant but not brown. Add the shrimp in one layer and cook 3 to 4 minutes, turning once, until pink and just opaque. Squeeze in the lemon juice, scatter with parsley and serve.
